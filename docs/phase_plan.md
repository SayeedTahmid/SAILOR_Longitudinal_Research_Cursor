# SAILOR Phase Plan

Only one phase may be active in a response. No phase is batched with another, and the next phase starts only after explicit approval of the current phase gate.

1. **Phase 1 — Data Foundation (CURRENT):** deliver the architecture reading, CPU-only canonical audit implementation, sections 01–09, confound procedure, and gap-only download policy; **gate:** synthetic verification passes, then the Colab audit must verify provenance and adjudicate G1/G5/G7/G8/G9/G10 before any Phase 2 data work.
2. **Phase 2 — Deterministic preprocessing:** create versioned MNI-space preprocessing, normalization from measured G10 statistics, valid locked masks, longitudinal windows, fresh patient-level CV manifests, and fresh-runtime QC; **gate:** outputs are reproducible, leakage-free, non-degenerate, splits are frozen, and preprocessing heterogeneity is documented.
3. **Phase 3 — Baseline floor:** run persistence C−1, MRI-only C0, and MRI+`Δt` C1 under frozen nested patient-level CV with patient-bootstrap CIs; **gate:** G3 persistence comparisons and G4 `Δt` ablation are complete and minimum detectable effects are reported.
4. **Phase 4 — Spatial encoder:** verify obtainable/licensed checkpoints, select the frozen cached primary encoder and one fallback, and compare against from-scratch 3D CNN/Swin under the fixed protocol; **gate:** G6 passes, cache provenance is complete, and the encoder choice is approved without outcome-driven switching.
5. **Phase 5 — Longitudinal encoder:** add irregular-`Δt` temporal history modelling using the fixed spatial features and shape contracts; **gate:** A1 and timing tests pass under patient-level CV or the temporal module is dropped and the negative result retained.
6. **Phase 6 — Treatment conditioning:** run the pre-specified MI/time-only diagnostics, C2/C3, and P1/P2/P3 with `unknown` as missingness and verified dose prerequisites; **gate:** G2 is adjudicated exactly as pre-specified and failed status/dose claims are explicitly prohibited.
7. **Phase 7 — Fusion:** compare cross-attention with concatenation using the winning fixed conditioning set; **gate:** A2/A7 show a supported paired patient-level gain or cross-attention is dropped.
8. **Phase 8 — Residual formulation:** compare residual prediction with direct future prediction while holding data, folds, and conditioning fixed; **gate:** A3 shows a supported gain or residual modelling is dropped.
9. **Phase 9 — Conditional diffusion:** add the simplest justified conditional diffusion variant and empirically profile it on target hardware; **gate:** A4 beats the non-diffusion residual model within measured VRAM/runtime limits or diffusion is dropped.
10. **Phase 10 — Full C4 assembly:** assemble the surviving modules for C4 and compare C4 with C1/C2/C3 without rescuing any failed component control; **gate:** complementarity/redundancy is adjudicated with paired patient-bootstrap CIs and no treatment claim exceeds G2 evidence.
11. **Phase 11 — Architectural ablations:** complete A0–A7 under the same frozen nested patient-level CV, including frozen/fine-tuned and foundation/baseline comparisons; **gate:** all pre-specified ablations, paired tests, multiplicity handling, and negative results are complete.
12. **Phase 12 — Final statistical evaluation:** aggregate outer-fold per-patient results, uncertainty, calibration, timing sensitivity, and cross-implementation checks without session-level inference; **gate:** all confidence intervals, guard outcomes, limitations, and reproducibility records pass an internal statistical audit.
13. **Phase 13 — Figures and reproducibility package:** generate final tables/figures, model/data cards, environment manifests, fresh-runtime dashboard verification, and code-to-result traceability; **gate:** every reported value resolves to a clean commit, frozen manifest, completion record, and reproducible command/notebook section.
14. **Phase 14 — Manuscript drafting:** write methods, results, limitations, negative findings, and narrowly supported contributions with verified citations only; **gate:** target lock, G2/G3/G7 caveats, patient-level uncertainty, and all `[VERIFY]` references pass co-author review.
15. **Phase 15 — Pre-submission audit:** run an adversarial scientific, leakage, provenance, statistical, reporting, and venue-compliance review and freeze the release candidate; **gate:** every blocking issue is resolved or explicitly disclosed and all authors approve submission.
16. **Phase 16 — Submission:** archive the immutable submission artefacts and submit the approved manuscript and allowed supplementary materials; **gate:** submission receipt, archived version identifiers, and post-submission change log are recorded before any follow-up work.
